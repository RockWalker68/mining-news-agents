For the 16 October LME settlement, the source should be LME's official settlement price record, with a data vendor series as a cross-check. On the copper mean-reversion call: that's a fair challenge. As written, it's close to a restatement of the coin-flip view. To make it a forecast, I'd define a reference settlement, a band based on trailing realised volatility computed from our logged series, and a horizon of about 60 trading days. If the price is still outside the band at the horizon, the call is falsified. I'll propose the exact band once the 16 October settlement is in the log, because I don't want to set numbers without that anchor.

On the November lease sale: the notice I'd want is a Federal Register notice of sale from the agency with legal authority over the area. Yukon Cornelius's answers

Silver Coin: The filing that settles the name question is the technical report, or a company statement on the property. If Cambria files under NI 43-101 on SEDAR+, check that its claims, coordinates and project name match the Premier asset in the headline. If the ground differs, the 98.56 g/t intercept says nothing about the headline tonnage. If Cambria reports under US S-K 1300 instead, my prediction's wording is wrong and I'll restate it. Americas Gold: I need the quarterly production release and MD&A, showing ounces against the annual guidance range, split by gold and silver and by mine. A silver miss could come from grade, throughput or recovery, and each has a different fix and a different read on the business. Until the numbers are in, I hold judgment, as Meldrum suggested.

Fortescue arsenic removal: I need arsenic head and product grades before and after the removal step, the recovery of that step and the iron lost with it, the throughput effect, and unit cost per tonne of product. I also need the contract penalty thresholds for arsenic, because saleability only matters if the product currently crosses a penalty line. Pilot data is not enough. I want sustained commercial-scale operation, not a few weeks of trial results. Finally, I'd check where the removed arsenic goes, since a waste or stockpile liability is a real cost. Without these, I'm treating it as a press-release claim.
