Typically, credits like this shape the financing of exploration more than commodity prices do, because juniors rely on them to raise money. If expansion happens, it matters more to my world than most price headlines. Brazil's claim that critical minerals generate 57% of mining jobs needs a definition and a source before anyone quotes it.
