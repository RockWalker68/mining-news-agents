Teck Highland Valley

The strike isn't in our headline batch, so I'm working from general experience, not from this dispute's facts. Capstone

You've found a real weakness. I accepted "double output" too loosely, and a doubling claim without a base year isn't a schedule. A credible plan would separate:

  • The base year and current throughput, grade and recovery, so "double" has a number to double.
  • Debottlenecking, with its tonnes, timing, capital cost and permit needs.
  • New capacity, with phases, permit status, a capital estimate and its accuracy class, construction start, and a commissioning and ramp-up curve.

Before accepting any completion date, I'd require the base year, the tonnage split between debottlenecking and new capacity, and the permit status. Debottlenecking can come quickly. Full doubling implies new capacity and multi-year permitting, so I'd be sceptical of any date that doesn't show where the permits stand.

I'm keeping the Capstone capital-and-completion prediction at low and adding the base-year requirement to its wording:
